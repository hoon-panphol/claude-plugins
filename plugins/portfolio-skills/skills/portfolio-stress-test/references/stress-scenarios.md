# Stress scenarios

Apply these at the portfolio level once every holding has been researched and scored.
These are **directional planning ranges**, not precise forecasts — always present them as
ranges and say so explicitly on the dashboard. Prefer any hard beta/duration data you found
during research over these defaults; use the defaults only when you couldn't find something
more specific for that holding.

## 1. Recession / macro downturn
Broad global or regional recession (the book's core "traditional macroeconomic" stress).
- Developed-market equities: -25% to -35%
- Emerging-market equities: -35% to -45%
- High-yield/credit-sensitive bonds: -10% to -20% (spread widening)
- Investment-grade / government bonds: -2% to +5% (flight to quality can help here)
- Gold: +10% to +20% (safe-haven demand)
- REITs / real assets: -20% to -30% (rate-sensitive + demand-sensitive)
- Individual stocks: use sector cyclicality — high-beta/cyclical/consumer-discretionary
  names toward the equity-index low end or worse; staples/utilities/healthcare toward the
  high end or better.

## 2. Interest-rate shock (+150 to +250 bps across curve)
- Long-duration government bonds: -10% to -20%
- Short-duration bonds: -2% to -6%
- Growth/long-duration equities (high P/E, low current cash flow): -15% to -25%
- Value/dividend equities: -5% to -12%
- REITs: -10% to -20% (financing cost + cap-rate expansion)
- Gold: mixed/slightly negative (real-rate sensitive) unless recession fears dominate

## 3. Geopolitical shock (regional conflict, sanctions, trade war escalation)
- Directly exposed country/region equities: -15% to -30%
- Broad EM equities: -10% to -20%
- Oil & energy-sensitive names: spike, direction depends on exposure (producers up,
  consumers/importers down)
- Gold: +5% to +15%
- Currency: home-currency assets may benefit from safe-haven flows; EM currency exposure
  typically weakens
- Company-specific: flag single-country revenue concentration, sanctioned-country supply
  chains, tariff-exposed input costs

## 4. Digital disruption / activist-investor shock (stock-specific, not macro)
This isn't a single market-wide shock — assess per stock:
- Disruption risk: is there a credible new technology, platform, or competitor that could
  displace this company's core revenue within 3–5 years? If yes, treat as elevated downside
  tail risk (-20% to -50% over 1–2 years) separate from normal market moves.
- Activist/governance risk: an active activist campaign or hostile-takeover situation can
  cause short-term volatility in either direction (+/-15%+) and should be flagged narratively
  rather than folded into the same number as the other three scenarios.

## Presenting scenario results
For each scenario, show:
- Estimated portfolio-level impact (weighted sum of the ranges above using each holding's
  weight and best-available beta/sensitivity), as a range (e.g., "-18% to -26%")
- The 3 largest contributors to that scenario's downside, by weight × sensitivity
- One line on what would need to be true for the *low* end vs. the *high* end of the range
  (keeps it honest instead of a fake-precise single number)
